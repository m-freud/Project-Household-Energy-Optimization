## 3.1 Original Dataset

### Origin

data/dataset_original/

The project is based on the data from [this paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352340923003372?via%3Dihub) from june 2023.

It represents a European residential community of 250 households, including energy consumption from the grid, two electric vehicles (EVs), photovoltaic generation (PV) for 200 households, and battery storage systems for 150 households.


### Format

The Dataset is stored as EC_EV_dataset.xlsx and contains technical lookup tables as well as time series data for a 24h-day, with a resolution of 96 steps that are 15 minutes apart.

Table Descriptions can be found in General description.docx

Data from the source are loaded into an SQLite database at sqlite/source_original.db .


Tables:

- *base_load*
    96-step time series

    Initial load consumption before PV generation and charging of BESS or EVs, taken from 'Load' table.

    One 96-step column per household_id

- *base_load_metadata
    Metadata calculated for each hh's load profile: 
    - total energy: total consumption over the day in kwh
    - max_load
    - std_load: standard deviation
    - load_factor: max_load / total energy
    - peak period
    - morning_mean
    - evening_mean
    - ramp_std: std of change between timesteps
