# 1. Overview

HEMS is a rapidly growing and evolving industry [insert statistics].

Conscious management of energy flows can save consumers a lot,
especially with a whole bunch of devices. A HEM is only as good as the decision making behind it.

At the same time, hardware like PV panels are getting cheaper every year.

Adding devices like heat pumps and PVs and EVs gives end consumers real benefits
and its worth checking out how to help them get the best value out of it.

etc

Autonomy, sustainability, ...

optimizing energy flows between devices has never been more relevant

two main challenges:
managing energy flows as best as possible with the data/information we have about the system

and

gathering best possible information.
this means gathering current data, and more crucially, predicting future Inputs or behaviour of devices or consumers

this project is an exercise in 
- simulating a set of 250 households with multiple devices
- comparing a set of control strategies over the population, under different scenarios

we show that employing MPC with a basic machine learning predictor can significantly lower cost over the day, as controllers are able to make better decisions.

With the use of MPC we can solve the problem of decision making in a mathematical way,
and the true quality bottleneck is the forecasting. (Given a perfect knowledge of the future, we can calculate a perfect control behaviour)
Even without user-specific knowledge, we can use ML technologies to greatly improve our decision making.
